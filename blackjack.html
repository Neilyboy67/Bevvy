System tag values & method

Bet ramp uses Hi-Lo equivalent True Count (RPC ÷ 2, RAPC ÷ 4) and the active system. Aces marked side are tracked separately and do not affect the running count for that system.

SystemA23456789T

Bet ramp (per box, Hi-Lo eq. TC): TC<1→1u, TC<2→1u, TC<3→2u, TC<4→4u, TC<5→6u, TC<6→8u, TC≥6→10u. Capped at ~1% bankroll per box. Total bet = boxes × per-box.

How to use

Tap each card as it is dealt — to you, the dealer, or any other box. The running count and recommended bet update live. Switch active system by tapping it. Reset the shoe when the dealer shuffles.

Camera mode opens the rear camera as a backdrop so you can keep the table in view while tapping. There is no automatic card recognition.
